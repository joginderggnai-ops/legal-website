FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Careers at Resurgent Legal — answers to the questions applicants ask us most.

Send Your Application

How do I apply for a position at Resurgent Legal LLP?

Send your updated CV along with a brief introduction and your preferred practice area to info@resurgentlegal.com. Mention the role and location you are interested in. If no specific opening is listed, we still welcome applications — we review every submission and reach out when a suitable position opens.

Does Resurgent Legal offer internships for law students?

Yes, we offer internships for law students in their 3rd year and above across our offices in Gurgaon, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Ludhiana, Jaipur, and Ahmedabad. Interns work on live client matters alongside our lawyers, gaining hands-on exposure to corporate advisory, transactions, insolvency, litigation, and regulatory work. Apply with your CV, a writing sample, and your preferred dates and location.

What practice areas can I work in at Resurgent Legal?

We hire across all eight practice areas — Corporate & Commercial, Mergers & Transaction Advisory, NPA & Stressed Assets Resolution, Foreign Investment & FEMA, Employment & Labour Laws, Insolvency & Bankruptcy, Start-Up Assistance, and Litigation & Dispute Resolution. Associates typically gain exposure to multiple areas rather than being siloed into one.

What experience level do you hire for?

We hire at all levels — fresh law graduates (0–2 years), mid-level associates (3–5 years), and senior lawyers (5+ years) with domain expertise. We also welcome lateral moves from other law firms, in-house legal teams, and regulatory bodies. Each role is matched to your experience and the practice area's needs.

Which office locations have openings?

Opportunities are available across our seven offices — Gurgaon (head office), Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Ludhiana, Jaipur, and Ahmedabad. Specific openings vary by office and practice area. If you have a location preference, mention it in your application and we will match you to available roles in that city.
