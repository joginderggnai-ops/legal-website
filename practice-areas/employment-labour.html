What should an employment agreement include in India?

Designation, compensation, probation, notice period, non-compete/non-solicitation, confidentiality, IP assignment, termination conditions, and dispute resolution. We draft with inbuilt safeguards enforceable under Indian law.

What is POSH compliance and is it mandatory?

Mandatory for every organisation with 10+ employees. Requires constituting an Internal Complaints Committee, awareness workshops, displayed policy, and annual reports. We handle end-to-end POSH compliance.

What are the key labour law changes businesses should know?

India's four new Labour Codes consolidate 29 existing laws — revised overtime limits, universal social security, fixed-term employment, and stricter safety standards. We help businesses update their compliance.

Can non-compete clauses be enforced in India?

During employment — generally yes. Post-employment — largely unenforceable under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act. Non-solicitation and garden leave provisions offer practical protection. We draft to maximise enforceability.

What registrations does a new business need under labour laws?

Shops & Establishments Act, Factories Act, EPF, ESI, Professional Tax, Labour Welfare Fund, and Contract Labour Act — depending on state and industry. We identify and obtain all applicable registrations.
