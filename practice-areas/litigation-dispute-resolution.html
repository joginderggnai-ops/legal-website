Frequently Asked Questions

What is pre-litigation advisory?

Assessing a dispute before filing — evaluating strength, outcomes, costs, and alternatives. Often prevents expensive litigation by identifying settlement opportunities. We recommend it for every dispute.

What disputes does Resurgent Legal handle?

Commercial disputes, shareholder disputes, contractual disagreements, real estate, employment disputes, regulatory enforcement, and white-collar crime defence — domestic and cross-border.

Arbitration vs litigation — what is the difference?

Litigation is in public courts with formal procedures and appeal rights. Arbitration is private, confidential, with party-chosen arbitrators and final binding awards. Arbitration is generally faster.

Can you handle international arbitration?

Yes — ICC, SIAC, LCIA, and ad hoc rules. We also advise on enforcement of foreign awards in India under Part II of the Arbitration Act, 1996.

When should I brief a senior counsel?

For Supreme Court, High Court, or tribunal hearings in complex or high-stakes matters. Senior counsel bring courtroom authority and specialised expertise. We identify the right counsel, prepare briefs, and coordinate the engagement.
