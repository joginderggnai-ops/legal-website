What is a willful defaulter and what are the consequences?

A borrower who has capacity to repay but deliberately defaults or diverts funds. Classification bars institutional credit, triggers criminal proceedings, and imposes director disqualification. We defend against wrongful classification.

What defences are available against ED and CBI proceedings?

Challenging procedural irregularities, demonstrating legitimate transactions, seeking bail, challenging attachment orders, and filing writ petitions. We build comprehensive strategies based on the facts of each case.

What is the difference between IBC and debt recovery proceedings?

IBC focuses on resolution as a going concern with moratorium protection. Debt recovery (RDDBFI/SARFAESI) focuses on recovering dues through asset enforcement. We advise on the strategically appropriate route.

Can a guarantor be taken to IBC?

Yes, since 2018 amendments, personal guarantors to corporate debtors can face insolvency proceedings under Part III. We represent guarantors, challenging unwarranted claims and protecting personal assets.

What is CIRP?

Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process — a time-bound process where an insolvency professional manages the debtor, invites resolution plans, and presents them to the Committee of Creditors. NCLT supervises. We represent all parties.
