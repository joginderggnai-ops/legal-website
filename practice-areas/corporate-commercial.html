Frequently Asked Questions

What does a corporate lawyer do for a business in India?

A corporate lawyer advises on entity incorporation, governance, regulatory compliance, contract drafting, and day-to-day commercial legal matters. Our corporate team handles everything from company registration and board governance to licence applications and commercial agreements.

How can a law firm help with corporate compliance?

We conduct corporate health checks to assess your compliance posture across company law, SEBI regulations, RBI guidelines, and sector-specific rules. We close gaps, obtain missing registrations, and set up ongoing compliance calendars.

What is included in corporate secretarial services?

Corporate secretarial services cover board meeting management, statutory filings with the ROC, maintenance of statutory registers, annual return preparation, and compliance with the Companies Act, 2013.

When should a business hire a corporate lawyer?

Ideally before you incorporate. For existing businesses, engage a corporate lawyer when entering new contracts, raising capital, expanding jurisdictions, or facing regulatory scrutiny.

What types of contracts does Resurgent Legal draft?

We draft supply contracts, distribution agreements, service agreements, licensing deals, franchise agreements, NDAs, and memoranda of understanding — all tailored to protect your commercial interests.
