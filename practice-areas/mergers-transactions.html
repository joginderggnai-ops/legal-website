What is the role of a legal advisor in M&A?

A legal advisor structures the deal, conducts due diligence, drafts transaction documents (SPA, SHA, BTA), negotiates terms, obtains regulatory approvals, and manages closing and post-closing integration.

What is legal due diligence in M&A?

A comprehensive review of the target's corporate records, contracts, litigation history, regulatory compliance, IP, employment matters, real estate titles, and pending liabilities to identify risks affecting deal value.

What approvals are needed for mergers in India?

Depending on the transaction — NCLT, CCI, RBI, SEBI, and sector-specific regulators. Our team identifies all required approvals early and manages the filing process.

What is a share subscription agreement?

A contract under which an investor subscribes to newly issued shares, covering investment amount, share price, conditions precedent, representations and warranties, and exit rights.

How long does an M&A transaction typically take?

A straightforward acquisition may close in 2–3 months. Complex transactions involving NCLT or CCI clearances can take 6–12 months.
