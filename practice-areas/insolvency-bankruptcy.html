What is the IBC and when does it apply?

The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 applies when a corporate debtor defaults on ₹1 crore or more. Financial creditors, operational creditors, or the debtor itself can initiate proceedings before the NCLT.

Resolution plan vs liquidation — what is the difference?

A resolution plan revives the debtor through new management, debt restructuring, or asset sale. Liquidation sells assets to repay creditors when no viable plan is approved. Resolution is always preferred under the IBC.

Who can be a resolution applicant?

Any person, but Section 29A disqualifies willful defaulters, undischarged insolvents, NPA-connected persons, and disqualified directors. We conduct Section 29A compliance checks for prospective applicants.

What is pre-insolvency advisory?

Assessing options before formal IBC proceedings — restructuring, OTS with lenders, strategic stake sales, asset monetisation, and contingency planning. Early advisory often avoids formal insolvency.

How long does CIRP take?

180 days, extendable by 90 (total 270). The Supreme Court holds 330 days as the outer limit including litigation. We ensure compliance with statutory deadlines.
